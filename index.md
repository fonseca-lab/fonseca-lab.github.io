## Welcome

The Fonseca Lab studies how cancer-driving DNA alterations shape the immune microenvironment of prostate cancer. We apply genomic profiling and spatial imaging technologies to probe how cancer cells with specific driver gene alterations communicate with the immune cells that can promote or inhibit cancer growth. Our projects are anchored in human prostate cancer biology, leveraging unique biobanked and clinically defined patient cohorts to ensure clinical relevance. Our goal is to build a combined genomic and immune framework that will refine how immunotherapies are developed and selected for patients with metastatic prostate cancer.

We also study a rare inherited form of kidney cancer caused by inactivation of the tumour suppressor gene von Hippel-Lindau (VHL). Our lab proudly hosts the BC Cancer von Hippel Lindau Biobank. Our on-going research delves into the spatial organisation of the VHL-associated immune microenvironment and the immune correlates of response to belzutifan treatment. 

The lab is situated at the Deeley Research Centre (part of BC Cancer-Victoria) on beautiful Vancouver Island. Our translational research benefits from close ties with colleagues at the University of Victoria, the Vancouver Prostate Centre, Vancouver General Hospital and BC Cancer-Vancouver.
