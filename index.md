## Welcome

### 
The Fonseca Lab is situated at the Deeley Research Centre (part of BC Cancer-Victoria) on beautiful Vancouver Island. Our research group studies how cancer driving DNA alterations shape the immune microenvironment of prostate cancer. We apply genomic profiling and spatial imaging technologies such as DNA sequencing, multiplexed immunofluorescence and spatial transcriptomics to probe how tumours with specific driver gene alterations communicate with the immune milieu. Our goal is to build a combined genomic and immune framework that will refine how immunotherapies are developed and selected for patients with metastatic prostate cancer.

We also study a rare inherited form of kidney cancer caused by inactivation of the tumour suppressor von Hippel Lindau (VHL) gene. Our lab proudly hosts the BC Cancer von Hippel Lindau Biobank. Our on-going research in this area delves into the spatial organisation of the VHL-associated immmune microenvironment and the immune correlates of response to belzutifan treatment.
