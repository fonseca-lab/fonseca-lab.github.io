## Welcome

### 
The Fonseca Lab is situated at the Deeley Research Centre (part of BC Cancer-Victoria) on beautiful Vancouver Island. Our research group studies how cancer driving DNA alterations shape the immune response. Focusing on prostate and kidney cancers, we aim to build a combined genomic and immune framework that improves how immunotherapies are developed and selected for patients.
